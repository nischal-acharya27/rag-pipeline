ACME Corporation Employee Handbook
Last Updated: January 2026

Chapter 1: Welcome to ACME

Welcome to ACME Corporation! We are glad you have chosen to join our team. This handbook is designed to provide you with information about working conditions, employee benefits, and policies affecting your employment. You should read, understand, and comply with all provisions of the handbook. It describes many of your responsibilities as an employee and outlines the programs developed by ACME to benefit employees.

No employee handbook can anticipate every circumstance or question about policy. As ACME continues to grow, the need may arise to change policies described in the handbook. ACME reserves the right to revise, supplement, or rescind any policies or portion of the handbook as it deems appropriate, in its sole and absolute discretion.

Chapter 2: Employment Policies

2.1 Equal Opportunity Employment

ACME Corporation is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, genetic information, veteran status, or any other legally protected class. This policy applies to all terms and conditions of employment, including recruiting, hiring, placement, promotion, termination, layoff, recall, transfer, leaves of absence, compensation, and training.

2.2 At-Will Employment

Employment with ACME is at-will. This means that either the employee or the company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause, and with or without notice. Nothing in this handbook or in any document or statement shall limit the right to terminate employment at-will.

2.3 Background Checks

All offers of employment are contingent upon the successful completion of a background check. ACME will conduct background checks in accordance with applicable federal and state laws. The type and extent of the background check will depend on the position being filled.

Chapter 3: Compensation and Benefits

3.1 Pay Schedule

Employees are paid on a bi-weekly basis, every other Friday. Direct deposit is available and encouraged. If a payday falls on a holiday, employees will be paid on the last business day before the holiday.

3.2 Health Insurance

ACME provides comprehensive health insurance coverage to all full-time employees starting on the first day of the month following 30 days of employment. Coverage includes medical, dental, and vision plans. The company pays 80% of the premium for employee-only coverage and 60% of the premium for dependent coverage.

Employees may choose from three medical plan options:
- PPO Plan: Broad network, higher premiums, lower out-of-pocket costs
- HMO Plan: Smaller network, lower premiums, requires referrals for specialists
- HDHP Plan: High deductible, lowest premiums, compatible with Health Savings Account (HSA)

3.3 Retirement Plan

ACME offers a 401(k) retirement savings plan to all employees who have completed 90 days of employment. The company matches 100% of the first 3% of salary contributed and 50% of the next 2% of salary contributed, for a maximum company match of 4% of salary. Employees are immediately vested in their own contributions and become fully vested in company matching contributions after three years of service.

3.4 Paid Time Off (PTO)

Full-time employees accrue PTO based on their length of service:
- 0-2 years: 15 days per year (accrued at 1.25 days per month)
- 3-5 years: 20 days per year (accrued at 1.67 days per month)
- 6+ years: 25 days per year (accrued at 2.08 days per month)

PTO can be used for vacation, personal time, or sick leave. Employees may carry over up to 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year. Any PTO balance exceeding the carryover limit will be forfeited on January 1.

3.5 Parental Leave

ACME provides 12 weeks of paid parental leave to eligible employees for the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. Employees must have been employed for at least one year and worked at least 1,250 hours in the preceding 12 months to be eligible. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the qualifying event.

Chapter 4: Workplace Policies

4.1 Remote Work Policy

Eligible employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Remote work arrangements must be formally requested and approved through the HR portal. Employees working remotely are expected to maintain their regular work schedule and be available during core business hours (10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their local time zone).

Remote employees must have a dedicated workspace with reliable internet connectivity (minimum 25 Mbps download speed). The company provides a one-time $500 stipend for home office setup and an annual $200 allowance for internet and utilities.

4.2 Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to conduct themselves in a professional manner. This includes treating colleagues, clients, and vendors with respect and courtesy. Harassment, discrimination, bullying, and retaliation of any kind will not be tolerated and may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

4.3 Confidentiality

During employment, employees may have access to confidential and proprietary information belonging to ACME Corporation. Employees must not disclose, publish, or otherwise reveal any confidential information to third parties during or after their employment without prior written authorization. All confidential materials must be returned upon termination of employment.

4.4 Use of Company Technology

Company-provided technology including laptops, phones, and software licenses are the property of ACME Corporation and should be used primarily for business purposes. Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work responsibilities, violate any company policy, or compromise system security.

All data stored on company devices is subject to monitoring and review. Employees should have no expectation of privacy regarding information stored on or transmitted through company systems.

4.5 Professional Development

ACME encourages continuous learning and professional growth. Employees may expense up to $2,500 per calendar year for professional development activities including courses, certifications, conferences, and books. All professional development expenses must be pre-approved by the employee's manager and must be relevant to the employee's current role or career path within the company.

Chapter 5: Leave Policies

5.1 Federal Holidays

ACME observes the following federal holidays:
- New Year's Day (January 1)
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day (Third Monday in January)
- Presidents' Day (Third Monday in February)
- Memorial Day (Last Monday in May)
- Juneteenth (June 19)
- Independence Day (July 4)
- Labor Day (First Monday in September)
- Veterans Day (November 11)
- Thanksgiving Day (Fourth Thursday in November)
- Day after Thanksgiving (Fourth Friday in November)
- Christmas Day (December 25)

When a holiday falls on a Saturday, it is observed on the preceding Friday. When a holiday falls on a Sunday, it is observed on the following Monday.

5.2 Family and Medical Leave

Eligible employees may take up to 12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave per year under the Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) for qualifying events including the birth or placement of a child, caring for a family member with a serious health condition, or the employee's own serious health condition.

5.3 Bereavement Leave

Employees are granted up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, domestic partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent, or grandchild) and up to 3 days for other family members.

5.4 Jury Duty

ACME supports employees who are called for jury duty. Employees will receive their regular pay for up to 10 days of jury service per calendar year. Employees must provide a copy of the jury duty summons to their manager as soon as possible after receiving it.
